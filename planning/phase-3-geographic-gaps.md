# Phase 3: Geographic Gaps & Future Development

## Purpose
Identify geographic blind spots in each companion book. These are candidates for future expansion, Vol III, or regional case study series.

---

## Companion 1: Remote Sensing for Environmental Modelling

### Current Coverage
- **Optical RS examples:** Temperate forests, boreal, agricultural areas
- **SAR examples:** Mostly Canadian examples
- **LiDAR:** Boreal forest, mountain terrain
- **Thermal:** Limited examples
- **Hyperspectral:** Research-level only; limited operational examples

### Geographic Gaps

| Region | Gap | Future Opportunity |
|--------|-----|---|
| **Tropical regions** | Rainforest remote sensing (cloud cover, dense canopy) | Case study: Amazon deforestation detection; cloud-robust RS methods |
| **Semi-arid/arid** | Dust, sparse vegetation, water stress detection | Case study: Desertification monitoring; irrigation efficiency from space |
| **Polar regions** | Sea ice, permafrost, melt dynamics | Case study: Arctic change detection; seasonal ice mapping |
| **Coastal/marine** | Water optics, coral bleaching, harmful algal blooms | Case study: Coastal ecosystem monitoring; water quality from space |
| **Urban** | Built-up area mapping, heat islands, impervious surfaces | Case study: Urban expansion monitoring; slum mapping from RS |
| **Monsoon regions** | Seasonal flooding, inundation dynamics | Case study: Flood inundation mapping; agricultural calendar from phenology |

### Synthetic Chapters Needed
- RS for tropical systems (cloud correction, dense vegetation)
- Water optics & coastal RS
- Urban-scale RS applications
- Seasonal/monsoon dynamics from satellite

## Companion 2: Cryosphere & Mountain Systems

### Current Coverage
- **Snow/glacier:** Canadian Rockies, BC glaciers, Arctic
- **Mountain hydrology:** Alpine, boreal context
- **Avalanche/debris:** Canadian ranges emphasis
- **Permafrost:** Canadian Arctic primarily
- **Hazards:** North American focus

### Geographic Gaps

| Region | Gap | Future Opportunity |
|--------|-----|---|
| **Tropical glaciers** | Andes, East Africa, Indonesia — unique physics | Case study: Kilimanjaro, Central Andes glacier retreat; tropics have different energy balance |
| **Himalayan systems** | Monsoon-driven snow, GLOF hazards in densely populated areas | Case study: Hindu Kush-Himalayan water security; monsoon timing impacts |
| **Patagonian** | Maritime glaciers, rapid change | Case study: Southern Patagonia retreat; sea-level rise contributions |
| **New Zealand Alps** | Temperate maritime context | Case study: Southern Alps; lake outburst hazards in tourist areas |
| **Central Asian ranges** | Water towers for downstream agriculture; permafrost | Case study: Tianshan water resources; permafrost-driven hazards |
| **European Alps** | Tourism, ski industry impacts; avalanche management | Case study: Alpine resort planning; climate change impacts on winter sports |

### Synthetic Chapters Needed
- Tropical glacier physics (different radiation regime)
- Monsoon-influenced snow/precipitation
- Transboundary water security (Hindu Kush, Andes, Central Asia)
- Economic/social implications of cryosphere change
- Avalanche risk management in populated areas

## Cross-Cutting Geographic Gaps

### Systematic Issues
1. **Global North bias** — Heavy Canada/US/Europe focus; limited Africa, South Asia, Southeast Asia, Pacific
2. **Data availability bias** — Examples cluster where Landsat/Sentinel data are freely available and processed
3. **Climate zone bias** — Temperate focus; limited tropical, arid, polar applications
4. **Development context bias** — OECD economies dominate; limited Global South, informal economy examples
5. **Institutional bias** — Examples from well-resourced institutions; limited community/NGO perspectives

### Regions Significantly Underrepresented
- **Sub-Saharan Africa** — Only mentioned in ML gaps
- **South Asia** (India, Bangladesh, Pakistan) — Limited except monsoon hydrology
- **Southeast Asia** — Only economics/trade
- **Pacific Islands** — Almost absent except sea-level rise
- **Central Asia** — Only brief Himalayan mention
- **Amazonia** — Only forest loss (not integral systems)
- **Latin America (non-Andean)** — Minimal coverage
- **West Africa** — Not covered at all
- **Informal economies** — Underrepresented everywhere

## Future Development Strategy

### Priority Tiers

**Tier 1 (High Impact):** Cases that bridge methods to policy/application
- Sub-Saharan African contexts (50% of world's poorest, high climate vulnerability)
- South Asian monsoon systems (affects 2B people)
- Southeast Asian rapid land-use change
- Tropical glacier retreat (impacts on water security)

**Tier 2 (Methods Extension):** Cases that test methods in new contexts
- Data-scarce environments (alternative data sources)
- Extreme climate zones (tropical, polar)
- Real-time forecasting for disaster risk
- Informal economy systems

**Tier 3 (Broader Context):** Cases that add perspective without heavy lifting
- European Alps (ski industry, tourism)
- Australia (semi-arid, fire, water)
- Japan (aging society, aging infrastructure)
- New Zealand (maritime, tourism)

### Suggested Vol III or Case Study Series

**Option A: Regional Deep-Dives**
- "Computational Geography of the Monsoon: South Asia & Southeast Asia"
- "Remote Sensing for African Land Systems"
- "Mountain Systems of the World: Tropical to Polar Glaciers"
- "Geopolitics of Energy Infrastructure"

**Option B: Thematic Extensions**
- "Data-Scarce Geographies: Methods for the Global South"
- "Informal Economies: Trade, Resources, Labour"
- "Climate-Driven Migration: Modeling Human Movement"
- "Coastal Risk & Island Vulnerability"

**Option C: Hybrid (Companions + Case Studies)**
- Keep 4 companions as universal methods/foundations
- Add 4-6 regional case study companions focusing on Tiers 1-2
